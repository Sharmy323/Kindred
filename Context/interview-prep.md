# Interview Prep

> Don't just read this — **draft your own answers in writing.** The value is in the reps.

## Likely question themes

### "Why TRM / why crypto compliance?"
Have a genuine answer. Weak: "crypto is exciting." Stronger: tie it to a real interest in
financial crime, trust & safety, regulated markets, or a mission of making crypto safe
enough to be usable. Name something specific TRM does that you find compelling.

### Domain understanding
- Walk me through what happens when a suspicious transaction is detected. (alert →
  triage → investigate → SAR)
- What makes attribution hard? Why is it TRM's moat?
- How would you reduce false positives without missing real threats?

### Product / analytical thinking (if PM-adjacent)
- How would you prioritize which new blockchain to support next?
- A compliance customer says they get too many alerts. How do you diagnose and fix it?
- Design a feature to cut investigation time for an analyst. What's the metric?

### Behavioral (STAR — Situation, Task, Action, Result)
Prep 4–5 stories that flex to multiple prompts:
- Drove a decision with ambiguous/incomplete data.
- Influenced without authority / handled a stakeholder conflict.
- A failure and what you changed afterward.
- Shipped something end-to-end under a real constraint.

## Sharp questions to ask them

Good questions signal you understand the business. A few starters:

- How does the team balance automation/risk scoring against the explainability that
  regulated customers need?
- What's the hardest part of adding coverage for a new chain — engineering, or attribution?
- How do you measure the quality of a risk score internally? What does "good" look like?
- Where does the product roadmap feel most contested right now?
- Who is the primary user you optimize for — the compliance analyst, the investigator, or
  the customer's leadership buying the platform?
