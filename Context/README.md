# TRM Labs — Interview & Company Research

A small research repository to prep for a TRM Labs interview. Everything here is a
working draft — **verify company specifics (funding, headcount, leadership, exact
product names) against [trmlabs.com](https://www.trmlabs.com) before relying on them.**

## Contents

| File | What's in it |
|------|--------------|
| [company-overview.md](company-overview.md) | What TRM does, market, customers, business model |
| [products-and-tech.md](products-and-tech.md) | Product suite, how the tech works, the data problem |
| [domain-glossary.md](domain-glossary.md) | Crypto-compliance / blockchain-intelligence terms |
| [interview-prep.md](interview-prep.md) | Likely questions, STAR stories, questions to ask |

## How to use this

1. Read `company-overview` and `domain-glossary` first to get fluent in the language.
2. Skim `products-and-tech` so you can talk about *how* the product actually works.
3. Draft your answers in `interview-prep` — don't just read it, write your own.

> ⚠️ **Verification flags:** Anything marked `[VERIFY]` is something I'm not confident
> is current. Check it before the interview.
