# Product Manager Take-Home Exercise - Anish Sharma

# Objective

We want to understand how you identify high-value product opportunities, reason through ambiguous product decisions, and leverage AI to create meaningful customer value.

This exercise is intentionally open-ended. We're less interested in whether you arrive at the "right" answer and more interested in how you identify customer problems, prioritize opportunities, make thoughtful tradeoffs, and communicate your thinking.

Rather than optimizing an existing workflow or automating a manual task, we encourage you to think about how AI can fundamentally change what customers are capable of accomplishing.

# Background

TRM builds investigative software that helps organizations combat financial crime, cyber threats, fraud, sanctions evasion, and national security risks.

Historically, TRM has been best known for blockchain intelligence, helping customers trace cryptocurrency transactions and investigate illicit activity. Today, TRM is evolving into an AI-powered investigative platform that helps customers reason across many forms of investigative data, automate complex workflows, and make better decisions.

For this exercise, assume TRM's platform already has access to a broad range of investigative information, including cryptocurrency transactions and wallets, people and organizations, sanctions and watchlists, corporate records, cyber infrastructure (e.g. domains, IPs, malware), social media, messaging platforms (e.g. Telegram), dark web intelligence, public reporting, and other open-source and commercial investigative data.

Your focus should be on how AI helps customers create more value from this information.

# Customer Segments

TRM serves customers across multiple markets. While each has different missions, they all rely on investigative data to make high-impact decisions.

| Customer Segment | Primary Goal | Example Outcomes |

# Your task

You do not need to solve for every segment. Choose one customer segment and one specific user within that segment.

Starting with that customer, identify a valuable opportunity where AI could meaningfully improve how they achieve their goals. We are interested in how you reason from customer and problem to solution not just the final concept. A focused product solving one important problem is stronger than a broad platform vision. Assume your solution can leverage TRM's investigative data and AI capabilities described above. Focus on the product experience and customer value rather than expanding the underlying data available.

Your submission should:

1. **Choose a customer segment** and briefly explain why you believe it represents a valuable opportunity.
2. **Identify a meaningful customer problem** by focusing on a real bottleneck or ceiling in how the user gets to outcomes they care about today.
3. **Prioritize an initial AI-native solution** that fundamentally improves the user's decision-making, output, or scope of work.
4. **Communicate your solution** through a prototype and product brief that could be handed to engineering, product marketing, and design to begin implementation.

Your proposal should be realistically scoped as an initial product, with clear tradeoffs and intentionally omitted functionality.

We are not looking for a feature that simply automates an existing workflow. We are looking for a product that meaningfully changes what the user is capable of accomplishing.

# Deliverables

Assume these materials will be handed to engineering, design, and product marketing to begin building your product.

## 1. Prototype

Create a functional and interactive prototype that communicates your proposed product experience.

Your prototype should demonstrate:

- The primary user workflow
- The core AI-powered experience
- How the user accomplishes their goal differently than they do today
- The end-to-end product experience

Focus on clearly communicating your product concept rather than producing polished visuals. We care more about the quality of your thinking than the fidelity of the prototype.

Use any prototyping tools you'd like.

**Optional reimbursement**

If you'd like access to a paid AI tool (e.g. ChatGPT, Gamma) while preparing, TRM will reimburse one month of usage (up to $20 USD). Please submit your receipt to **talent@trmlabs.com**.

## 2. Product Brief (2 pages max)

Imagine this document will be handed to engineering, design, and product marketing, and you won't be available to answer follow-up questions.

Your brief should provide the context behind your prototype, including:

- The customer you chose and why you prioritized them
- The problem you're solving and why it's important
- Your proposed solution and why AI is necessary
- The key assumptions, tradeoffs, and functionality you intentionally chose not to build
- What you would build first and how you would measure success

Feel free to include any additional context you believe is important. We're interested not only in the quality of your solution, but also in how clearly you communicate your product decisions.

# Presentation

## Introductions (5 minutes)

Meet the interview panel.

## Prototype walkthrough (20 minutes)

Walk us through:

- Why you chose this customer segment
- The user and the problem they face
- Your product experience
- How AI is used
- The key tradeoffs you made
- What you intentionally chose not to build

We'll ask clarifying questions throughout.

## Product brief review (15 minutes)

Please provide your product brief as a Google Doc before the interview.

Following your walkthrough, we'll spend approximately 10 minutes reviewing the document silently.

Assume the brief must stand entirely on its own.

## Live product iteration (15 minutes)

We'll introduce a new customer insight, business constraint, or technical limitation that changes the problem.

Using AI and your prototype, you'll iterate on your solution in real time.

The goal isn't to arrive at a perfect answer. We're interested in how you:

- Incorporate new information
- Make tradeoffs under constraints
- Leverage AI to accelerate your thinking
- Communicate your reasoning as the product evolves
