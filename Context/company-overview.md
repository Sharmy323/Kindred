# Company Overview

## What TRM Labs does

TRM Labs is a **blockchain intelligence** company. It builds software that helps
organizations detect and investigate financial crime involving cryptocurrency —
money laundering, sanctions evasion, fraud, terrorist financing, and hacks/exploits.

The core idea: public blockchains are transparent ledgers, but raw on-chain data is
pseudonymous and enormous. TRM converts that raw data into risk signals and
investigative tooling — connecting wallet addresses to real-world entities (exchanges,
mixers, sanctioned actors, scam operations) and scoring transactions for risk.

## Who buys it

- **Financial institutions / banks** exploring or offering crypto exposure.
- **Crypto exchanges and VASPs** (Virtual Asset Service Providers) that need AML/KYC
  and transaction monitoring to stay compliant.
- **Government & law enforcement** agencies running investigations and sanctions
  enforcement. `[VERIFY]` — public reporting has associated TRM with US federal agencies.
- **Fintechs and payment companies** touching digital assets.

## Why it exists (the market)

Regulators increasingly hold crypto businesses to the same AML/CFT standards as banks
(Bank Secrecy Act, FATF Travel Rule, OFAC sanctions). A VASP that can't monitor
transactions or screen sanctioned wallets faces enforcement, fines, and loss of banking
relationships. TRM sells the tooling that makes compliance and investigation tractable.
