How it started

Bank records
Contacted on WhatsApp by someone posing as an investment advisor. Three weeks of trust-building before any money moved. Scammer showed fake returns on a fake trading app. Told Margaret not to tell her family.
Source: victim statement and case notes (Carmen Reyes).

How payments escalated

Bank records
Five wire transfers over six weeks. Started at $5,000, grew to $25,000. Each one followed a phone call. Money was wired to a third-party bank account Margaret had never used before.
Source: transaction history (Pacific National Bank).

Where the crypto went

TRM blockchain data
The third-party account converted Margaret's money to USDT at a US exchange. From there, TRM traced it through two intermediate wallets to a Southeast Asian exchange with weak identity checks. All five deposits followed the same path to the same wallet cluster.
Source: TRM wallet attribution and fund tracing.

Unusual account activity

Bank records
New payee added after 4 years of unchanged activity. Transaction amounts 3x her previous maximum. Late-night banking logins coinciding with phone calls. No prior crypto activity.
Source: account history (Pacific National Bank).
